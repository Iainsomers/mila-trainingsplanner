MiLa Planner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Who we are

MiLa Planner is operated by Oaxley Trading BV, Utrechtseweg 20, Zeist, The Netherlands ("MiLa Planner", "we", "us" or "our").

For privacy questions or requests, contact Iain Somers at iainsomers@yahoo.com.

2. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to athletes, coaches and other users of MiLa Planner, including users who use training-planning features and athletes who choose to connect an Oura account.

3. Personal data we process

Depending on how you use MiLa Planner, we may process:

  • Account information, such as your name, email address, login details and account role.
  • Training and planning information, such as training plans, sessions, comments and goals.
  • Competition information, such as competition dates, distances and athlete selections.
  • Wellness information that you enter, such as sleep duration, perceived sleep quality, morning heart rate and HRV.
  • Oura connection information, if you choose to connect your account, including the authorisation tokens needed to maintain your connection. If Oura data import is enabled, this may also include daily sleep, activity or readiness summaries and heart-rate information, depending on the permissions you approve.
  • Technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as login and access logs.

We will not use this information for purposes other than providing and operating MiLa Planner as described in this policy.

4. How we use your information

We use personal data to:

  • provide and maintain MiLa Planner;
  • show training plans, competition information and wellness information to the relevant athlete and that athlete’s authorised coaches;
  • connect an Oura account and, when data import is enabled, synchronise the information you authorise; and
  • operate, protect and troubleshoot the service.

We do not use personal data for advertising, sell it, or use it for unrelated purposes.

5. Oura connection

The Oura connection is optional. If you choose to connect, Oura will ask you to sign in and authorise specified data permissions. MiLa Planner will not receive your Oura password. The Oura test window can temporarily retrieve up to 14 recent days of sleep duration, sleep and readiness scores, lowest sleeping heart rate and average sleep HRV for preview. Preview results are not saved to MiLa Planner or added to Daily Vitals.

You can disconnect Oura in the Hardware settings panel. Disconnecting removes the stored connection tokens and stops future access.

Oura processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Who can see your information

Your information may be viewed by you and the coaches you have authorised in MiLa Planner. It is not visible to other athletes. There are no exceptions to this access rule.

7. Legal grounds for processing

We process ordinary account and planning information as necessary to provide the MiLa Planner service you use.

For wellness or Oura information that qualifies as special-category health data, we will only process it when an applicable legal condition has been established and, where required, you have given explicit consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by contacting us or disconnecting Oura, where applicable. Withdrawal does not affect processing that took place before consent was withdrawn.

8. Service providers

MiLa Planner is hosted on Render, which also hosts the production database. Oura provides data when you authorise the Oura connection.

MiLa Planner loads the Bootstrap user-interface framework from jsDelivr. When your browser requests these files, jsDelivr may receive standard technical connection information, such as your IP address.

9. How long we keep information

We keep account, training, competition and wellness information while your MiLa Planner account is active. If you request account deletion, we delete or anonymise this information within 30 days, except where the law requires us to retain specific information for longer. Encrypted backups may retain deleted information for up to 90 days before it is overwritten.

If you disconnect Oura, future synchronisation stops and the connection tokens are removed. Previously synchronised Oura information remains until you delete your account or request deletion of that information.

10. Security

We use safeguards designed to protect personal data, including secure HTTPS connections, account authentication and access controls intended to ensure that athletes can access only their own information and coaches can access only information for athletes they are authorised to coach.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to certain processing, to receive a copy of certain data, or to withdraw consent.

To make a request, contact iainsomers@yahoo.com. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. In the Netherlands, this is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

12. Children and young athletes

MiLa Planner may be used by athletes under 18.

Children under 12 may use MiLa Planner only under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian. An athlete under 18 may connect an Oura account only with the permission of a parent or legal guardian.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when MiLa Planner or its data practices change. The current version will be published at mila-trainingsplanner.onrender.com/privacy/. We will update the “Last updated” date when we publish a revised version.
